BABCOCK UNIVERSITY LOST & FOUND SYSTEM (FINDIT)

COURSE CODE: BU-COS 209

PROJECT NAME: FindIt

• 1. INTRODUCTION

  • 1.1 Purpose of The Product Requirements Document (PRD)

  This Product Requirements Document (PRD) outlines the requirements, objectives and scope of the proposed FindIt System.

  It explains the problem the system is intended to solve, the needs of its users and the features the system should provide. It also serves as a guide for the development team and relevant university personnel, ensuring that everyone involved understands what the system is expected to do.

  The document will guide the development of a system that makes reporting, managing and recovering lost items easier within the Babcock University community.

  • 1.2 Product Overview

  The FindIt System is a digital platform that makes it easier for students and the Babcock community to report, hold and recover lost items.

  Our platform will allow students to report missing items, while authorized university personnel will be able to manage all items submitted to the FindIt office.

  The system helps connect reports of missing items with records of recovered items, making it easier for university personnel to identify the rightful owners and contact them.

  University personnel will remain responsible for keeping recovered items safe, verifying ownership and releasing them to their rightful owners. Students will collect their items from the FindIt office after providing the required identification and proof of ownership.

  The system will support the university's existing Lost & Found process by improving communication, item management and the recovery of lost items.

  • 1.3 Background and Context

  At Babcock University, students who lose personal items often rely on WhatsApp groups, or they ask people around the area where the item was last seen. These methods are good for spreading information on missing items, but they do not provide a central platform where reports can be submitted and duly followed up.

  When recovered items are handed over to university personnel, they are recorded and kept at the FindIt office. However, there is no established method for connecting the records of missing items to the found items at the FindIt office, making it difficult to identify and contact the rightful owners.

  The university previously had a Lost & Found system, but it was discontinued several years ago. The specific reasons for its discontinuation are unclear. Since then, the process has relied on posting on WhatsApp departmental groups and the existing physical procedures handled by university personnel.

  These challenges showed us the need for a more organized approach that connects students with missing items to the personnel managing recovered items. The FindIt System aims to provide this connection through a centralized platform while maintaining the university's existing physical collection process.

  • 1.4 Problem Statement

  Students mostly rely on WhatsApp groups or asking people around to report or search for lost items. However, these methods are not well organized, and most students receive little or no response and follow-up after reporting their missing items.

  University personnel also face difficulties identifying the owners of recovered items. Although they record and keep these items at the FindIt office, there is no centralized way to match them with students' reports.

  Our FindIt System aims to solve this problem by providing a central platform where students can report missing items and university personnel can record recovered items, identify possible owners and contact them.

• 2. PRODUCT VISION AND OBJECTIVES

  • 2.1 Product Vision

  To create a centralized platform where students can report lost items and university personnel can easily identify owners and return found items.

  • 2.2 Product Goals

  1. Provide students with a central platform to report missing items.
  2. Allow university personnel to record and hold found items.
  3. Make it easier to match reports of missing items with records of found items.
  4. Notify the relevant university personnel whenever a student submits a report.
  5. Improve communication and follow-up between students and university personnel.
  6. Ensure that ownership is verified through official means and recovered items are released.

  • 2.3 Expected Outcomes

  The FindIt System is expected to bring the following improvements:

  1. Students will have a simpler way to report lost items.
  2. University personnel will be able to keep track of found items more easily.
  3. It will be easier to connect reports of missing items with items that have been found.
  4. Students will receive follow-ups and updates on their reports.
  5. More lost belongings will have a better chance of reaching their rightful owners.

• 3. USER RESEARCH AND ANALYSIS

  • 3.1 Research Methodology

  We conducted face-to-face interviews with 10 participants: 7 students and 3 university personnel.

  The students were asked about their experiences with losing belongings.

  The university personnel were asked how they receive, record, store and return items that have been found.

  The responses helped us understand how the current process works, identify the challenges faced by both groups and determine areas that need improvement.

  • 3.2 Target Users

  The Babcock University FindIt System is intended for two main groups of users:

  1. Students: Students will use the platform to report lost items, providing as much detail as possible about missing items, and receive updates and follow-ups about their reports.

  2. University Personnel: University personnel will use the platform to record found items, manage those items, review possible matches with lost-item reports and contact potential owners. They will also remain responsible for verifying ownership and releasing recovered items to their rightful owners.

  • 3.3 Research Findings

  The interviews with students and university personnel revealed the following:

  1. Students rely on posting on departmental WhatsApp groups and asking people around the area where the item was last seen.
  2. Most students receive little or no response and follow-up after reporting their lost items.
  3. University personnel record details of found items. However, they do not have an established way to identify and contact their owners.
  4. Found items are kept at the FindIt office. Before collecting an item, a person must provide identification and proof of ownership.

• 4. PRODUCT SCOPE

  • 4.1 In-Scope Features

  The first version of the FindIt System will include the following features:

  1. It would allow users to create accounts and log in to the system.
  2. It would also allow users to report lost items by providing as much detail as possible to identify the items.
  3. It would allow university personnel to record newly recovered items.
  4. It would allow university personnel to view, update and manage lost and found item records.
  5. It would notify the relevant university personnel whenever a new lost-item report is submitted.
  6. It would help university personnel compare lost-item reports with records of found items to identify possible matches.
  7. It would allow users to receive updates about their reports.
  8. It would ensure that university personnel confirm a person's identity and proof of ownership before releasing a recovered item.

  • 4.2 MVP Definition

  The first version of the FindIt System will focus on the basic features needed to report and recover lost items.

  Users will be able to create accounts, log in and report lost items, while university personnel would be able to record found items, manage reports, receive notifications and identify possible matches.

  Users will also receive updates about their reports, while university personnel will remain responsible for verifying ownership and releasing recovered items.

  The system can be improved with additional features in the future.

• 5. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

  • 5.1 User Registration and Login

  The system shall:

  1. Allow users to create accounts using their UMIS details.
  2. Allow registered users to log in securely.
  3. Allow users to log out of their accounts.
  4. Restrict access to authorized university personnel features to approved personnel and prevent any unauthorized user from accessing the system.

  • 5.2 Lost Item Reporting

  The system shall:

  1. Allow users to submit reports of lost items.
  2. Allow users to provide as much detail as possible when describing the items and upload images if available.
  3. Save reports, notify university personnel and allow users to check the status of their reports.

  • 5.3 Found Item Registration

  The system shall:

  1. Allow university personnel to register items that have been found.
  2. Allow personnel to enter details about the item.
  3. Save the records and update them when necessary.

  • 5.4 Notifications

  The system shall:

  1. Notify university personnel whenever a new lost-item report is submitted.
  2. Notify users when there is an update on their reports.
  3. Notify university personnel when a user submits a claim for a found item for collection.

• 6. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

  The system shall:

  1. Be secure enough to protect users' accounts through secure login, restrict access to unauthorized users and personnel, and store information safely.
  2. Have a simple and clear layout for users to report lost items and track the status of their reports. It should also be responsive on both laptops and phones.
  3. Load pages and display information within a reasonable timeframe, allowing users to search for and view items smoothly.
  4. Be accessible to users and university personnel and be available on both mobile phones and computers with an internet connection.

• 7. SUCCESS METRICS

  The success of the system will be measured by:

  1. The speed and ease with which users can report lost items.
  2. The number of lost items returned to their owners.
  3. The response time of university personnel.
  4. User satisfaction, based on whether they find the system helpful and easy to use.

• 8. CONSTRAINTS, ASSUMPTIONS AND RISKS

  The system will require an internet connection and a device to work. It will also depend on university personnel recording found items and verifying ownership.

  Possible challenges include poor internet access, limited resources, delayed responses and false claims on recovered items.

  These challenges can be reduced through proper system management, secure accounts and proper ownership checks.

• 9. CONCLUSION

  The proposed FindIt System aims to make reporting and recovering lost belongings easier and more organized.

  By providing a central platform for reporting lost items, recording found items and improving communication, the system will help students and university personnel manage lost and found belongings more effectively. Overall, the system is expected to improve the chances of reuniting lost items with their rightful owners.
